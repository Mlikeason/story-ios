A NOTE FROM THE MAKER

Keeping a moment.

A photograph reminds me what a place looked like. A sound brings back what it felt like to be there: rain outside a window, a fan turning in a warm room, distant voices in the afternoon.

I started recording these small moments because I wanted to keep a little of them. Story is a place to return to those recordings — a private travel journal you can listen to.

Some recordings were made with a professional audio recorder. Others were captured on a phone because the moment happened unexpectedly.

These are field recordings, with all the imperfections that come with them. Wind may brush the microphone. A voice may pass through. The sound is not always clear or perfectly balanced. Those details belong to the moment, too.

I try to make each recording comfortable to listen to while keeping its character. What matters to me is that it still sounds like somewhere I was, at a time that will never happen quite the same way again.

Perhaps something here will bring you back to a place of your own.

把那一刻留下来

照片让我记起一个地方的样子，声音却常常让我想起，身处那里是什么感觉。窗外的雨，闷热房间里转动的风扇，午后远处的人声。

我开始录下这些小小的片刻，是想留住其中的一点什么。后来做了 Story，让自己可以随时回到这些声音里。它是一本可以听的私人旅行手记。

这里有些声音是用专业录音机录的，而有些则因为事发突然而仅仅是用手机录制的。

这些录音并不完美。风可能擦过麦克风，偶尔有人声走近，声音也未必总是清晰、均衡。它们来自真实的现场，那些不够漂亮的细节，同样属于那一刻。

我会尽量把录音处理得适合慢慢听，同时留下它原有的样子。对我来说，珍贵的是它确实发生过：在某个地方，某一段不会原样重来的时间里。

也许，这里的一段声音，也会把你带回自己的某个地方。
