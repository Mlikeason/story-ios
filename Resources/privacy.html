Privacy policy

Updated November 7, 2026

Story plays field recordings included with the app. You can listen offline without creating an account. The app does not automatically collect or upload personal data, track you, or use advertising or analytics services.

What stays on your device

Story saves your favorites, language, last recording, layout and sleep timer preferences on your device. The app and its widget share the language preference locally. The app sends track information to the system playback controls on your device. Your listening history is not sent to the developer.

Permissions

The sounds are prerecorded. Story does not access your microphone or location. Place names describe where the recordings were made.

Questions and deletion requests

Deleting Story removes its local preferences. Write to Story and problem reports open a draft in your email app; nothing is sent until you choose to send it. A problem report can include the current recording, timer and playback state, app version, device model and system version. You can edit or exclude these details before sending. No unique device identifiers or listening history are attached.

If you email zivzhu@gmail.com, the recipient receives your sender email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. They are used to respond to your request and improve Story. You can write to the same address to ask for the correspondence to be deleted. The Copy buttons write only the selected email address or draft to your device clipboard.

隐私声明

更新于 2026 年 11 月 7 日

Story 播放随 App 提供的实地录音，无需注册，可离线收听。App 不会自动收集或上传个人数据，不跟踪用户，也没有接入广告或统计服务。

保存在设备上的信息

收藏、语言、上次选择的录音、布局和睡眠定时偏好都保存在设备上。App 与小组件在本机共享语言设置，并把当前录音的信息提供给本机的系统播放控件。开发者不会收到你的收听记录。

设备权限

所有声音均为预先录制。Story 不会访问麦克风或定位。界面里的地名是录音地点。

联系与删除

删除 Story 会移除本机偏好。「写给 Story」和问题反馈会打开邮件草稿，只有你选择发送后才会寄出。问题反馈可以包含当前录音、定时和播放状态、App 版本、设备型号及系统版本，你可以编辑或取消附带这些信息。不会附带设备唯一标识或收听历史。

如果你主动发邮件到 zivzhu@gmail.com，收件人会收到你的发件邮箱，以及你选择发送的内容和附件，用于回复请求和改进 Story。你也可以写信到同一地址要求删除来信。复制按钮只会将所选邮箱或草稿写入本机剪贴板。
