A LITTLE HELP

Make yourself at home.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap the timer to choose looping, 25, 45 or 60 minutes. Pausing holds the time left; resuming continues the countdown. Changing recordings keeps that time. The last ten seconds fade out gently. Choosing a different duration starts a fresh timer.

Can I listen offline and with the screen locked?

All six recordings and their scenes are included in Story. Start a recording, then lock your screen. The system playback controls let you pause and resume. Calls or disconnecting headphones may pause playback.

Where is the playlist?

On a regular iPhone, use the small playlist button in the side column. On Duo's portrait cover, use the lower half of the layout switch. Unfold Duo to choose the full player or a split view. Portrait and tabletop layouts place the playlist below the scene. Tent mode keeps just the listening controls.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings to choose English or Simplified Chinese. On Duo, unfold the phone to reach Settings. Help and feedback are also at the bottom of the playlist.

What should I include in a problem report?

Tell me what you did, what happened and which recording was playing. Mention whether you used headphones or the speaker, and whether the phone was locked or folded. You can add a screenshot in your email app. If no mail app opens, copy the address and message into your preferred email service.
