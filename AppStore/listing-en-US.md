# English (U.S.)

**Name**  
Story: A Sound Journal

**Subtitle**  
Six places, captured in sound

**Keywords**  
rain,river,wind,white noise,nature,sleep timer,focus,offline,background,playlist,soundscape

**Description**

I’ve been making recordings on my travels for years. Story is a personal travel journal you can listen to. These six recordings are the first selection from that collection. I’ll continue adding new recordings and illustrations in future updates.

This first collection includes a river in Western Australia, rain in Changsha and an afternoon in a Singapore classroom. Each recording has a location, a year and an animated illustration. The recording from Changsha contains fifty minutes of rain.

Choose a recording and let it loop, even with the screen locked. Set a sleep timer for 25, 45 or 60 minutes, or keep listening without a limit. Pause and the timer waits for you. The last ten seconds gently fade into silence. The current scene appears on your Lock Screen.

On iPhone Duo, rest the phone in landscape to watch the waveform on the cover display. Open it for a full-screen player or a playlist beside your recording. Rotate the open phone to put the playlist below the scene.

All six recordings are included. Listen offline, without an account or ads. Choose English or Simplified Chinese in Settings.
